The algebra we commonly get's introduced to in school are some computational techniques for example how to add, multiply and divide two numbers or how to work with symbols. 

But algebra can also be an aesthetic subject Zeitz write. "*A good problem solver takes a more confident approach to algebraic problems. The wishful thinking strategy teaches to look for an *elegant* solution. Cultivate this mindset: employ a light, almost delicate touch, keeping watch for opportunities that avoid ugly manipulations in favor of elegant*." (p. 147)
